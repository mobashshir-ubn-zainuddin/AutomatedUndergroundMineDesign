Data & Calibration

The block model simulates an Archean lode gold deposit — gold occurring as free nuggets and fine particles within quartz veins, fundamentally different from disseminated base-metal deposits. Parameters are calibrated against published technical reports from real underground gold mines.

Gold-Specific Grade Model

Log-normal grade distribution with CV ~180% (gold typical: 100–300%, vs 40–80% for copper). Nugget effect modeled: ~2% of vein blocks receive coarse gold bonuses up to 30+ g/t. Calibrated against Red Lake (Evolution Mining) and LaRonde (Agnico Eagle) NI 43-101 published grade ranges. High-grade shoots at structural intersections, as observed in real Archean lode systems.

Economic Parameters

Gold price US$1,900/oz, mining cost $45/t, processing $25/t, G&A $10/t, 92% recovery — aligned with World Gold Council AISC benchmarks. Rock types modeled: Quartz Vein, Altered Basalt, Sericite Zone, Fresh Basalt — reflecting real alteration zonation around gold-bearing structures.

Geotechnical Inputs

Rock mass parameters (UCS 120 MPa, RQD ~65%, K-ratio 1.5) are representative of hard-rock Precambrian geology. Stope stability assessed using the Mathews method (Mathews et al., 1981; Potvin, 1988) — the global standard for underground open stope design.

How It Differs from Industry Practice

Traditional underground mine design relies on sequential manual workflows across disconnected software. This platform integrates the full chain into one automated, real-time pipeline.
